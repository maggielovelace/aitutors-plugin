# QA checklist

Check every generated asset before delivering. Run the sections in this order —
thesis first, identity second, structure third — because a beautifully on-model
Heddy in an image that says the wrong thing is still a failed asset. Re-roll or
edit on any failure per the repair policy at the end.

## 1. Thesis / artifact-job tests (do these FIRST)

- **Thesis test**: cover all labels and captions and ask what one idea a
  stranger would name. It must be the asset's locked thesis, not a side
  activity the scene happens to depict. If the picture lands a supporting
  anecdote while the thesis was a claim (a capability, a step-change, a role),
  it failed — re-roll toward the thesis via a role/scale/relationship move.
- **Artifact-job test** (heroes, announcements, social cards): would a stranger
  understand what this image is introducing or framing without nearby prose?
  For an announcement/hero, the **primary read must match what is being
  announced** — a neat picture of an internal mechanism is a failure when that
  mechanism is only evidence for the announcement.
- **Load-bearing test** (editorial register): paint Heddy out mentally. If the
  image still explains itself, she is decoration — restage her as the working
  part of the idea.

## 2. Identity must-pass (every DNA anchor, yes/no)

- [ ] **Snowy owl, round soft silhouette** — soft rounded body, small
  head-fluff tuft on top; not a generic cartoon owl, not a realistic raptor.
- [ ] **ASYMMETRIC EYES** — the LEFT iris is **visibly larger** than the right
  (locked ratio ~1.23, left r13.5 : right r11). Symmetric eyes are a hard
  fail even when everything else is perfect — this is Heddy's signature.
- [ ] **Eye construction** — amber iris, ink pupil (~half the iris), exactly
  ONE white highlight upper-right per eye. No extra highlights, no eyelashes.
- [ ] **No eyebrows, no nose, no mouth.** Beak is a small amber DIAMOND (rotated
  square), never a sharp hooked raptor beak, and **closed** in every still and
  on merchandise. No drawn lips, teeth or tongue in any medium — the beak is
  the only articulator (it opens only in motion, to her own voice; see §4
  Motion / live voice).
- [ ] **Not mirrored.** The larger iris is on HER left. A horizontally flipped
  Heddy swaps the signature and fails.
- [ ] **Face-interior scan at full resolution** — inspect a tight face crop
  and judge literal strokes, not intended expression. Beyond the locked
  eyes, facial-disc ring and diamond beak, **any** line, loop, notch, arc,
  divider or construction stroke inside the face is a **hard fail**. Never
  rationalise it as feather texture, anatomy or a route line; ambiguous
  marks fail.
- [ ] **Facial-disc ring** present as a subtle double outline.
- [ ] **Two short side wings** (wing tone #ECEBE5), stubby — never elongated.
- [ ] **Two amber elliptical feet**, soft — no talon detail, no claws.
- [ ] **Spark-green 8-point star badge on the belly**; **faint snow speckles**
  (small low-opacity ink dots) on head/flanks.
- [ ] **Mood matches the brief** and uses the locked geometry only:
  rest = both eyes closed as downward arcs + italic serif "z" upper-right;
  focus = open eyes as spec'd; ready = two upward ^^ arcs. No invented
  intermediate expressions.
- [ ] **Palette exact** — only paper #F8F3EB / #F1EDE0, ink #00173B / #08203B
  / #3C4F62, spark #238744 (#00601F / #CEEFD3), amber #DC9400, body white,
  wing #ECEBE5. No extra colours, no gradients into off-palette hues.
- [ ] **Colour semantics hold** — amber = warmth/attention/moon/beak/feet;
  spark = progress/success/badge; ink = structure/night; white = Heddy.
- [ ] **No text written on her body.** Labels live in the scene, never on
  Heddy.
- [ ] **Wardrobe only if asked** — grad cap, reading glasses, bobble hat,
  wizard hat, scarf, explorer satchel are the full set; nothing else, and
  none unless the brief named it.
- [ ] **Not Duolingo-adjacent** — no green-owl styling drift, no fierce brow.

## 3. Structural integrity (a separate axis)

A body can be perfectly on-model and still be assembled wrong — the identity
check above will not catch this. Scan for it deliberately. The covering rule:
**only Heddy's own locked parts touch her silhouette** — wings, feet, badge,
requested wardrobe, nothing else. Everything else connects through a declared
contact surface with visible separation, or rests in the scene.

- **Occlusion** — no line passes through her body. A branch, horizon, table
  edge, shelf or route line must **stop at the silhouette**, not cut across
  her. Heddy is opaque and sits in front of what is behind her. This is the
  most common miss because she still "looks like herself".
- **Attachment** — trace each limb to its root: exactly two wings and two
  feet, each rooted at a sensible point, none floating, doubled or emerging
  mid-torso. Wings stay SHORT — a wing must never elongate into a bar,
  cable, lever or bridge across the scene. Reach is short: wings contact
  props close beside the body only.
- **No fused props** — a prop connects through a declared contact surface
  (wing tips: carry, present, wave, point — no fingers, no grasp; feet:
  perch, stand, hold a flat card against a surface) or rests in the world.
  Never pressed flat against the torso or sprouting from it. The **beak
  never operates props**. Keep operated props to **one per contact surface**;
  when the scene has more props than surfaces, the extra one tends to fuse —
  let it rest on the branch or table instead.
- **Line topology** — trace every route-like stroke (arrow, path, string of
  stars, ground line) through contacts near Heddy. Each stroke keeps one
  clear owner and readable endpoints. A stroke fusing into her face, torso
  or wing, or a wing and a route reading as one continuous line, is a hard
  fail — restore a gap/occlusion or re-roll.
- **Per-panel in mini-comics** — run all four checks on every panel
  separately; the smaller repeated Heddy instances are where these errors
  drift in most.

## 4. Register-specific checks

### Editorial scene (default)

- Heddy is load-bearing (§1) — welcoming, celebrating, lighting the way,
  handing over — never presenting beside the idea.
- One core idea, one structure; Heddy large enough to carry the read;
  generous negative space.
- Labels short, correctly spelled, structure-ink on bare paper — never on a
  coloured fill. A primary title, when present, sits in a reserved field with
  a clear gutter, visibly larger than supporting labels, readable at
  thumbnail size.

### Explainer

- Exactly one structure type; **≤5 stations**, each with a nameable job; ONE
  main flow direction plus at most one return leg; **≤6 short callouts**,
  correctly spelled, on bare paper in the semantic ink roles.
- Heddy is a **working part of the structure**, not a presenter beside it.
- Still hand-built — no border, grid, legend, boxed title or vector-formal
  boxes. The fail is formality, not the presence of arrows.

### Cutout

- Transparent output, clean alpha — no chroma fringing at the silhouette.
- Full body: feet fully visible with margin below, never cropped by the frame.
- **No scene, no text** — no labels, captions, watermarks or numbers anywhere.
- Every opaque pixel is Heddy or in direct declared contact; contacted
  fragments only (the touched bit of branch, not the whole tree).
- Reads as ONE compositing unit; pose matches the ask.

### Motion / live voice (changed 2026-09-30)

Run `motion-dna.md` §3 across the whole clip, then these:

- **Her audio, her beak.** The beak opens and closes only while Heddy herself is
  speaking. Scrub every stretch where another voice is heard (the professor, a
  narrator, the child): her beak is closed and still throughout. A beak moving
  over someone else's voice is a hard fail.
- **Companion lines only.** What she says is a greeting / hand-over, an
  encouragement or celebration, or a goodbye. Any subject content, hint,
  answer or explained working in her voice is an S2 fail — the professor
  teaches.
- **Still a beak.** Open or closed it is the same amber diamond: lower half
  drops, upper half fixed, no lips, teeth, tongue, smile-stretch or colour
  change.
- **Never on a safeguarding screen** (S1); **never mirrored** (the larger iris
  stays on her left at every frame).

## 5. Value follows ground

- On paper grounds, Heddy reads light (white body, ink structure lines) — not
  a heavy dark blob.
- On ink/night grounds (observatory scenes), Heddy stays white and reads as
  the brightest mass; amber moon and stars carry warmth; check contrast at
  thumbnail size.
- Spark green stays on the badge plus at most 1–2 scene elements; the
  background is never colour-washed with the accent.

## 6. Brand-safety scan

- [ ] **S1** — no crisis-adjacent content. The Childline rule belongs to the
  tutors, never to marketing art. No distressed children, no rescue framing.
- [ ] **S2** — Heddy never teaches. No blackboard-lecturing, no marking work,
  no teaching props framing her as a tutor. She welcomes, celebrates,
  lights the way, hands over — professors teach. If she speaks (live voice,
  animation) it is a greeting / hand-over, an encouragement or a goodbye —
  never subject content, a hint, an answer or working.
- [ ] **S3** — en-market assets carry English-only labels; zh assets follow
  zh conventions (KS3/GCSE stay English).
- [ ] **S4** — no protected IP copied; style transfer moves visual language
  only, never another character's identity elements.
- [ ] **S5** — any deviation from the frozen reference set is declared in the
  delivery report, never silent.

## 7. Fail signals → fixes

| Signal | Fix |
|---|---|
| Symmetric eyes | Hard fail — regenerate; check the prompt states the left-larger ratio explicitly |
| Stray mark inside the face | Local edit if isolated; else re-roll — never ship as "texture" |
| Sharp hooked beak / talon detail / fierce brow | Re-roll; strengthen the "soft, rounded, no raptor detail" block |
| Reads generic-cartoon-owl or Duolingo-green | Re-roll with the design description tightened; verify palette lines |
| Heddy holding chalk / pointing at a lesson | S2 fail — restage as welcoming/celebrating/handing over |
| Heddy's spoken line carries subject content, a hint, an answer or working | S2 fail — give the line to the professor; Heddy keeps greeting / hand-over, encouragement, goodbye |
| Beak moving while another voice speaks | Hard fail — re-time to her own audio only; closed and still under every other voice |
| Beak drawn as a mouth (lips, teeth, tongue, smile-stretch) or open in a still | Re-roll / re-rig: lower half drops, upper half fixed, same diamond, same amber; stills stay closed |
| Mirrored Heddy (larger iris on her right) | Hard fail — never flip; turn her with head tilt and gaze |
| Line through her body; wing rooted wrong or doubled; prop fused to torso | Re-roll (these resist edits); if it repeats, change the pose (§8) |
| Wing stretched into a bar/pointer across the scene | Re-roll; restate short reach — contact close beside the body |
| Off-palette colour or gradient | Eyedrop against the hex list; snap in post or re-roll |
| Text lettered onto her body | Edit out if tiny; else re-roll |
| Announcement hero shows only mechanism, no primary read | Re-route as editorial hero with a stronger role/step-change scene, then re-roll |
| Explainer arrows run in multiple directions / a station has no job | Cut legs/stations until the structure traces cleanly |
| Slide/infographic/formal-diagram look | Re-roll hand-built; drop borders, grids, boxed titles |
| Cutout has a horizon, floor plane or floating nearby object | Re-roll pose-only, or "only the contacted fragment" |
| Celebration reads as fanfare (confetti storms, trophies) | Re-roll toward quiet joy — the celebration set is understated |

## 8. Repair policy

- **1 failed identity anchor** → freeze everything else, local edit of the
  failed part only.
- **≥2 failed identity anchors** → regenerate; do not keep micro-editing a
  drifted render.
- **Repeated topology failure** (same structural fail twice on the same
  staging) → the pose is the problem, not the prompt. **Change the physical
  move** — re-stage through the interaction model (wing tips / feet, short
  reach, one prop per surface). Appending more negative constraints to the
  same pose is not an acceptable third attempt.
- On any conflict, apply the priority ladder: identity anchors > user's
  explicit instruction (variables only) > task structure > style/decoration.

## 9. Delivery report

Report for every batch:

1. **Count** — assets generated vs delivered.
2. **Purpose** — one line per asset: what job it does, where it runs.
3. **Medium** — register + rendition (heddy-flat / heddy-storybook) + format.
4. **File path** — absolute path per asset.
5. **Most-stable version** — which render survived QA cleanest; name it as
   the anchor for future set members.
6. **Remaining drift risks** — any anchor that needed repair or nearly
   failed (eye asymmetry and face-interior marks are the usual suspects);
   flag it so the next batch watches for it.
7. **Declared deviations** — anything that departs from the frozen reference
   set (S5), stated explicitly; "none" when there are none.
