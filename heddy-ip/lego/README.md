# LEGO Heddy — engineered desk sculpture

**English** · [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

A buildable brick interpretation of Heddy, derived from the locked identity in
[`../skills/heddy-ip/references/heddy-dna.md`](../skills/heddy-ip/references/heddy-dna.md)
and the storybook turnaround. The workflow follows the Microduck approach:
**IP reference → character analysis → brick engineering → validated model →
assembly sequence → instructions → film**. The model is generated by code,
using real LDraw part geometry, and every render and step is produced from
one locked file: [`model/heddy.json`](model/heddy.json) (LDraw:
[`model/heddy.ldr`](model/heddy.ldr); it opens in Studio, LDCad or LeoCAD).

## 1. Character analysis (from `heddy-ip`)

Heddy is **one** soft egg: head and body are a single form with no neck. The
checks below come from the 13 locked anchors. The brief's recognition
priority (silhouette → face → eyes → colour → proportions → wings → feet)
decided every trade-off.

| Anchor | Source | LEGO translation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Silhouette | product path, 72 × 89 units | 62 plate layers sliced from the exact Bézier path; body 20 studs wide (1 IP unit = 5.57 LDU = 2.23 mm) |
| 2 One tuft | `M55.5 18 Q60 8.5 64.5 18` | one *Brick 2×1×1⅓ curved top* (6091), leaning like the reference |
| 3 White body | `#FFFFFF` | White; the whitest thing in frame |
| 4 Facial disc | faint second ring | the body front is flattened (owl facial plane). The studs-forward face panel sits 1.6 mm below it, so the disc reads as a faint shadow line, not a mask |
| 5 **Asymmetric eyes** | iris r 13.5 vs 11 | **Dish 8×8** vs **Dish 6×6** (ratio 1.33 vs 1.23: nearest real sizes; left clearly larger) |
| 6 Pupil + one highlight | ink pupil ≈ ½ iris, highlight upper-right | Earth Blue round plate 4×4 + three quarter-disc tiles; the fourth quadrant carries **one** white 1×1 round tile on the upper-right diagonal |
| 7 No brows, nose, blush, mouth | — | none. A panel seam that read like a mouth was engineered out (panel edges aligned to plate layers) |
| 8 Amber diamond beak | rotated square | 2×2 plates turned 45° on a jumper's single centre stud |
| 9 Short side wings | `#ECEBE5`, rooted at the sides, y 54–99 | Light Stone Grey leaf built into the flank layers, one stud proud of the body |
| 10 Amber pad feet | two ellipses, no talons | amber plates + two *curved slope 2×1* toes per foot |
| 11 Spark-green 8-point star | belly, r ≈ 7 | two Dark Green squares 45° apart (jumper 2×2 + turned tile 2×2) |
| 12 ~7 speckles | low-opacity ink | 7 Medium Stone Grey 1×1 round tiles on the crown and upper flanks |
| 13 Nothing else | — | nothing else on the body |

The brief asks for "eyebrow geometry". The IP overrides it: anchor 7 forbids
brows, and heddy-dna's priority ladder puts identity anchors above requests.
Heddy's expression comes from the asymmetric eyes and the highlight.

## 2. Colour mapping

Nearest LEGO colours by CIEDE2000 against the official LDraw colour table,
restricted to colours in current production:

| Heddy token | Hex | LEGO colour (id) | LDraw | ΔE00 | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| body white | `#FFFFFF` | White (1) | 15 | 0.0 | body, face, tuft |
| wing | `#ECEBE5` | Light Stone Grey (208) | 151 | 2.6 | wings |
| amber | `#DC9400` | Flame Yellowish Orange (191) | 191 | 10.3 | irises, beak, feet |
| ink-deep | `#00173B` | Earth Blue (140) | 272 | 9.1 | pupils |
| spark green | `#238744` | Dark Green (28) | 2 | 4.6 | belly star |
| ink @ 25 % (speckles) | `#BFC5CE` | Medium Stone Grey (194) | 71 | 9.1 | speckles, hidden structure |

Amber note: Earth Orange (ΔE 6.6) and Bright Yellowish Orange (7.0) are
closer but retired. Flame Yellowish Orange is the production amber and exists
in every element used here. Hidden structure is Medium Stone Grey, the same
as the speckles, so no extra colour enters the palette. The turntable is Dark
Stone Grey and is never visible.

## 3. Engineering

* **Shell:** each plate layer is the silhouette's cross-section (depth =
  0.9 × width, from the side view), rasterised to the stud grid. The shell is
  two studs thick and hollow inside. Five full floors tie the rings together.
* **Brick courses:** the near-vertical middle uses three-layer brick courses
  on a five-layer rhythm. That rhythm lines up with the face's SNOT rows
  (side-stud height = 14 LDU above a brick's base, so rows repeat every 5
  plates = 2 studs).
* **Terraces:** exposed treads on the dome are tiled, with no studs on the
  surface. True outline corners get quarter-round tiles.
* **Face:** a studs-forward panel (plates + tile skin) clicks onto
  *Brick 1×4 / 1×2 / 1×1 with studs on side*. Eyes stack on it:
  spacer round plates → radar dish (iris) → round plate 4×4 (pupil) → tiles.
* **Swivel:** a hidden 4×4 turntable splits the body just above the feet.
  The upper module (floor + three rings hung beneath it) is built
  upside-down, then pressed onto the turntable. This is what lets Heddy turn
  toward a notebook in the film. Head and body stay one form, as the IP
  requires, so there is no neck joint and no tilt.
* **Feet:** two 2×8 plates spanning front to heel, bridged by a 4×8 base
  plate. They carry the model alone; there is no stand.

### Validation (all pass — [`model/validation.md`](model/validation.md))

Collisions via an oriented-box separating-axis test · stud/anti-stud graph
(no floating parts, one connected structure) · build order (each part
connects to what is already built and slides in along its insertion axis
without passing through anything) · reverse disassembly · centre of mass
105.8 mm high, inside the feet's support polygon with a 31.7 mm margin (tips
only past 16.7°) · upper body turns ±20° without contact.

### Piece count

1,446 is above the brief's 600–1,200 guide. The count wasn't padded. About
310 pieces are hidden structure that a hollow 22 cm sculpted shell needs to
be buildable in order: the inner shell ring, floors, and the overhang
anchors at the base. Thinning the brick courses to one stud saved only 56
pieces and broke 23 build-order checks, and dropping floors *raised* the
count. Both options were measured and rejected.

## 4. Declared deviations

1. Iris ratio 1.33 instead of 1.23: the nearest real dish sizes.
2. The highlight sits on the upper-right diagonal at 0.35 × the pupil
   radius (IP: 0.77). Studs set the position.
3. The belly star uses two squares, so it is broader than the IP's slender
   points. Its eight points and colour are exact.
4. The speckles are 8 mm dots, larger than the IP's hairlines. There are
   seven of them.
5. Geometry for nine real elements was authored at true dimensions, because
   the vendored LDraw subset lacks them (98138, 60474, 87580, 25269, 27925,
   11477, 87087, 11211, 3403c01). Their underside detail is simplified.

## 5. Film

The three videos are rendered from the locked `model/heddy.json` (`tools/render_film.py`, then
`tools/make_film.py`). Every part enters by travelling along its own insertion axis and clicking
in on its landing frame. The only thing that moves on the finished model is the real
turntable swivel.

| File | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `video/heddy-lego-hero-16x9-4k.mp4` | 45 s, 3840×2160, 24 fps | the ten-shot storyboard: brick → build → face → reveal → exploded view → booklet → turn to the question card → brand |
| `video/heddy-lego-social-9x16.mp4` | 29.7 s, 1080×1920 | vertical re-cut, per-shot crops |
| `video/heddy-lego-sting-16x9-4k.mp4` | 8 s, 3840×2160 | eye click → HEDDY / heddy.app |

Frames were rendered at 1920×1080 with Cycles on CPU (10 samples + OpenImageDenoise), then
Lanczos-upscaled to 4K. The 9:16 cut is cropped from the same frames, and the stop-motion shots
run "on twos". Titles are set in Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans (OFL, `tools/fonts/`).
The sound is synthesised: one click per part landing, a quiet A-major-7 pad and a chime on the logo.

tools in the order above, then `render_film.py` and `make_film.py` for the films.
